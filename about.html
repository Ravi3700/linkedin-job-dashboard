Project Overview

The Hub brings together curated job-search links across career families and locations, alongside company career routes and job platforms. It is designed to help students, freshers and experienced job seekers explore opportunities without jumping between disconnected lists.

The public experience is a static site. A separate, optional content admin proposes search-list changes as GitHub pull requests; proposed edits do not publish until reviewed and merged.

Key Capabilities

Job DiscoverySearch roles, locations, recent vacancies, hiring posts and walk-in listings.
Career ResourcesCompany career routes, entry-level context and job-platform links.
Search FiltersFind searches by keyword and narrow supported results by Indian state.
Reviewed UpdatesOptional content changes are proposed through GitHub for human review before publishing.
